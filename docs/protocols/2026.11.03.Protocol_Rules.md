Relevant roles:
- App-modeler - a person who uses predefined GUI components to model their own
  app. They write GUI models.
- App-developer - an app-modeler, who additionally writes Java code to define
  custom application logic and test their application.
- Library-developer - a developer who implements Fenix RTE library
- Generator-developer - a developer who implements Fenix Generator

Each important part includes
- **Problem**
- **Example** with a current solution
- **Alternative** solution
- **Verdict** section

Each idea targets app/montigem-modellers and/or app/montigem-developers trying to make their job
easier.

Each **Verdict** section describes the decision that applies to a specific use
case, i.e. to the whole part after the previous **Verdict** section.

Approved with a date in the **Verdict** section means that the concept was
discussed with BR on that date and was accepted. In some cases a specific
alternative is specified as the approved one.
